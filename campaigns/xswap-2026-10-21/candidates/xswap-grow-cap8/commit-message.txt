mm: xswap: test an eight-chunk cap on metadata growth

Reference pilots executed 12 to 16 growth operations. Raising the cap
can satisfy a large occupancy deficit with fewer mapping episodes.
It does not force an eight-chunk allocation when less is needed.

Keep the occupancy target, device maximum clamp, stale-range checks,
allocation rollback, no-reclaim scope and publication ordering intact.
The experiment may allocate larger bursts or retain more metadata.
No throughput or latency improvement is assumed from fewer calls.

This independent candidate follows a smaller-shrink-minimum experiment
whose mixed retention results did not justify promotion.
