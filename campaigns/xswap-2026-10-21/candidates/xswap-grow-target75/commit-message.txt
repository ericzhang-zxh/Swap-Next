mm: xswap: test tighter headroom with the larger growth cap

The eight-chunk experiment reduced zswap growth episodes from 15-16 to
13, but neither retention nor tail latency justified adoption under the
observed A/A variability. Retain that cap and test a 75 percent growth
occupancy target instead of 65 percent to request less spare mapping.

The target remains between the 50 percent shrink trigger and 85 percent
grow trigger. Keep the chunk rounding, maximum clamp, stale-range
validation, locks, no-reclaim allocation and failure handling unchanged.

This trades less requested headroom for potentially more growth calls.
It is an evolution of the preceding experiment, expressed as a complete
independent patch against the same frozen reference. It is not a proven
performance improvement or a correctness certification.
