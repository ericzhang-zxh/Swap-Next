mm: xswap: test a four-chunk minimum tail shrink

The initial disk and zswap smoke observations retained 429 and 471 free
clusters at the mapped tail without a shrink. The eight-chunk minimum
and one-chunk slack require a larger free run in this configuration.

Lower the minimum to four chunks as a bounded metadata-retention
experiment. Keep the occupancy trigger, shrink target and slack intact.
The exact tail scan, locked revalidation, PCP invalidation, RCU wait,
backend release and allocation-failure fallback remain unchanged.

More frequent shrinking can increase unmap and grace-period costs and
can cause regrowth under oscillating demand. No performance improvement
or general correctness acceptance is claimed by this experiment.
