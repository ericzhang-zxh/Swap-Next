Why

Shrink only unmaps a contiguous free tail; one cache-only slot ends that tail.

Cache-only blockers explain the regime; the RAM involved is descriptor pages only

Per-cluster tables are freed with the cluster; high boots kept 37–50 nonempty clusters versus 37 in low boots.
